# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Busy people who skip meals or eat badly. They do not want to decide, plan or track; they want the next step done for them.

## Product Purpose
NutriFlow runs the whole daily food loop: it decides the next meal, fills the shopping list, guides cooking (voice-first, hands free) and logs what was eaten. Source of detail: CLAUDE.md.

## Positioning
The app decides and makes the next step one tap, instead of reporting numbers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Personal only in v1. Design tokens and rules live in CLAUDE.md (pastel palette, IBM Plex Sans, 20px gutter, 44px targets, min 12px text). Guest session or Google sign-in.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Decide for the user, make the next step one tap.
- Sleek and compact, no extra cards.
- Pastel selection; black only for the one main action.

## Brand Commitments
Name NutriFlow. Palette: mint, rose, sky, lavender, butter, peach.
